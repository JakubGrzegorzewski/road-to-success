**Advancement (próba)** – one person's plan for earning one rank: tasks, requirements and the mentor's notes. See [Creating and editing an advancement](/pomoc/proba).

**Rank (stopień)** – the scout or instructor rank the advancement is for (e.g. przewodnik, podharcmistrz). Every rank has its idea and requirements.

**Rank idea** – a description of who a person with this rank is. Tasks cover parts of it, which the app underlines.

**Requirement** – a specific condition of the rank, with a number (e.g. “5a”). Requirements are assigned to tasks.

**Initial and final requirements** – conditions for opening and closing the advancement (for ranks that have them).

**Task** – one thing to do in the advancement, with a status, requirements and a part of the idea. See [Tasks](/pomoc/zadania).

**Status** – the stage of work on a task: created, in progress, submitted, completed, failed. See [Task statuses](/pomoc/statusy).

**Mentor (opiekun)** – the person running the advancement, who leaves notes and assesses tasks.

**Mentee (podopieczny)** – the person doing the advancement.

**Comment (note)** – an entry on a task, a requirement or the whole advancement. It is open or resolved. See [Comments](/pomoc/komentarze).

**Room** – a group of devices that sync with each other. Access is given by a link with a key. See [Sync](/pomoc/synchronizacja).

**Backup (.rts)** – a file with all data that can be imported on any device. See [Backups](/pomoc/kopie-zapasowe).
