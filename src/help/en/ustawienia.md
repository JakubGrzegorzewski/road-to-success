Settings have their own tab. Everything you choose here applies to **this device only** – it is not synced to other devices.

## Language {#jezyk}

Polish or English. On a computer you can also change the language in the top right corner. Guides without a translation are shown in Polish.

## Writing as {#pisze-jako}

The person who signs the [comments](/pomoc/komentarze#pisze-jako) written on this device. Usually that is you – the mentor or the mentee. Set it once on every device. It is a label, not an account: anyone with the room link can write as anyone.

## Device name {#nazwa-urzadzenia}

The device name is in the **Sync** tab – other devices in the room see it. See [Sync](/pomoc/synchronizacja#nazwa-urzadzenia-i-lista-polaczonych).

## Backup {#kopia-zapasowa}

Exporting and importing backups, the JSON export and the copy from before joining a room are all described in [Backups](/pomoc/kopie-zapasowe).

## Device storage {#pamiec-urzadzenia}

Whether the browser may delete the app's data on its own, and the **Ask for persistent storage** button. Below it – how much space the data takes. More in [Offline use and privacy](/pomoc/offline-prywatnosc#trwale-przechowywanie).

## Privacy and version {#prywatnosc-i-wersja}

A short note on where the data lives and the app version number – useful when reporting a problem.
