A task is one thing the mentee will do in the advancement, e.g. “I will run a series of meetings about the Scout Law”. Every task has content, a [status](/pomoc/statusy), assigned requirements and a part of the rank idea.

![Task card](../img/zadanie.webp)

## Adding a task {#dodawanie-zadania}

Below the tasks tap “Add task”. A new card appears at the end, with a number and the status “Created”.

## Task content {#tresc-zadania}

Type the content into the large text field. It saves itself with every keystroke – there is no “Save” button.

> [!NOTE]
> The content can be written **on two devices at the same time**, e.g. the mentee on a phone and the mentor on a computer. Changes are merged character by character and nothing is overwritten, even when one device was offline for a while.

## Part of the idea {#fragment-idei}

Above the content there is the rank idea. Mark which part of it the task covers:

- **On a phone** tap the words one by one – they get selected.
- **On a computer** drag the mouse over a passage or click words.

Then choose “Save”. “Show the whole idea” removes the selection. Saved parts are underlined in the idea at the top of the advancement and in the PDF.

## Task requirements {#wymagania-zadania}

Below the content there are the rank's requirements:

- **On a wide screen** they are numbered buttons. Hover to see the requirement's text. A click adds or removes it (selected ones are highlighted).
- **On phones and tablets** expand “Requirements · selected: n” and tick the requirements – with their full text.

One requirement can be in several tasks. “Show selected requirements in tasks” at the top of the advancement shows which requirements have no task yet.

## Order {#kolejnosc}

Tasks are numbered in the order they were added: “Task 1”, “Task 2” and so on. The same numbers are used in the PDF and in comment references such as “Task 2”. Deleting a task shifts the numbers of the following ones.

## Deleting a task {#usuwanie-zadania}

At the bottom of the card there is “Delete task”. After confirmation the task disappears together with its comments – also on connected devices.
