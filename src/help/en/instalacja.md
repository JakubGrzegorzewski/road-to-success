Road to Success is a web app that can be installed like a regular app. It then has its own icon, opens full screen and works offline.

> [!TIP]
> On an iPhone installing has one more advantage: Safari will not delete the app's data even if you do not open it for a long time.

## How to install {#jak-zainstalowac}

:::ios
1. Open the app in **Safari** (other iPhone browsers may not offer this).
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and choose **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm with **Add**.
:::

:::android
1. Open the app in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu in the top right corner.
3. Choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. Confirm. The icon appears on the home screen and in the app drawer.
:::

:::desktop
1. Open the app in **Chrome** or **Edge**.
2. Click the install icon in the address bar (a screen with an arrow) or choose **Install Droga do sukcesu…** in the menu.
3. The app opens in its own window and appears in the Start menu / Launchpad.
:::

## Updates {#aktualizacje}

The app updates itself. A new version is downloaded in the background while you are online and starts the next time you open the app (or after refreshing the page). Your data stays unchanged. The version number is in [Settings](/pomoc/ustawienia).
