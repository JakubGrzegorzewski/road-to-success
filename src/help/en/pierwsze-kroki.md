Road to Success helps you plan and run an advancement towards a scout or instructor rank. The mentee writes tasks and assigns requirements and parts of the rank idea to them, the mentor leaves notes, and at the end the app turns it all into a PDF document.

![Advancement list on a phone](../img/lista-prob.webp)

## Where is my data {#gdzie-sa-moje-dane}

Everything is saved **on your device** – in the browser, without an account and without a data server. The app works offline too. To work on several devices (e.g. phone and computer, or you and your mentor), connect them with [sync](/pomoc/synchronizacja).

> [!TIP]
> If the data already exists on another device, **do not add anything again**. On the welcome screen choose “Join sync” or “Import a backup”. This way you avoid duplicated people and advancements.

## First advancement in 3 steps {#pierwsza-proba-w-3-krokach}

1. **Add yourself.** On the welcome screen choose “Add the first person” and type your name and rank. Later you add your mentor or mentees the same way (the “+” button in the advancement view).
2. **Create an advancement.** On the list tap “Add new advancement”. The app opens a new advancement for the first rank on the list.
3. **Set the details.** At the top of the advancement choose the rank, the mentee and the mentor. Then add the first task with “Add task” – see [Tasks](/pomoc/zadania).

## What next {#co-dalej}

- Working with a mentor? Connect your devices with [sync](/pomoc/synchronizacja) – you will both see the same tasks and [comments](/pomoc/komentarze).
- Install the app on your phone's home screen – [how](/pomoc/instalacja).
- Make a [backup](/pomoc/kopie-zapasowe) from time to time. The app will remind you.

> [!NOTE]
> Not sure what “rank idea” or “room” means? See the [glossary](/pomoc/slowniczek).
