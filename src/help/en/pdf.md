The app creates a PDF from the advancement's data – offline and without sending anything to a server.

![Document preview](../img/podglad-pdf.webp)

## Document preview {#podglad-dokumentu}

In the advancement view tap the round button with an eye in the bottom right corner (“Document preview”). The same button takes you back to editing. The preview shows exactly what goes into the PDF.

## Preview options {#opcje-podgladu}

### Layout: requirement table or task list {#uklad-tabela-wymagan-czy-lista-zadan}

- **Requirement table** – every requirement of the rank with the tasks that cover it. Good for checking that the advancement covers all requirements. On phones it is shown as cards.
- **Task list** – tasks one after another: content, status, part of the idea and requirements.

In both layouts the first page has the rank, mentee, mentor and the idea with underlined parts. The app remembers the chosen layout.

### Rank background {#tlo-stopnia}

“Rank background in PDF” puts the rank artwork (coloured band and logos) under every page. Without it the PDF is smaller and better for black-and-white printing.

### Comments in the PDF {#komentarze-w-pdf}

A PDF is usually a document to hand in, so **comments are not included by default**. Tick “Include comments” and choose:

- **Open only** – notes that are not resolved yet,
- **All** – resolved ones too (marked “resolved”).

In the list layout comments sit under their task, and general and requirement notes go to a “Notes” section at the end. In the table layout all comments are in the “Notes” section at the end, with references such as “Task 2”, “Req. 5”, “General”.

## Saving and sharing {#zapis-i-udostepnianie}

Choose “Download PDF”. What happens next depends on the device:

:::ios
1. The share sheet opens. Choose **Save to Files** to keep the document, or send it right away in Messages, mail or a messenger.
2. If the sheet does not appear, the PDF opens in a new tab – use its share button.
3. If creating the PDF took longer, a “PDF ready” window appears – tap **Share** or **Download**.
:::

:::android
1. The share menu opens – pick an app (e.g. Drive, Gmail, a messenger) or save to files.
2. If the menu does not appear, the file goes to “Downloads”.
3. If creating it took longer, choose **Share** or **Download** in the “PDF ready” window.
:::

:::desktop
The file is downloaded to your downloads folder, with a name like `przewodnik-jan-kowalski.pdf`.
:::

> [!TIP]
> The PDF works offline. If something goes wrong, see [troubleshooting](/pomoc/problemy#pdf-nie-pobiera-sie-na-iphonie).

## Printing {#drukowanie}

Download the PDF and print it from the program you open it in (the browser, Preview, Adobe Reader or the Files app on a phone). For black-and-white printing turn off “Rank background in PDF” first.
