Comments are a conversation between mentor and mentee next to a specific point of the advancement. Each note stays **open** until someone marks it as **resolved**. For both people to see the comments, connect your devices with [sync](/pomoc/synchronizacja).

![Comment thread on a task](../img/komentarze-watek.webp)

## Where to leave a note {#gdzie-zostawic-uwage}

| Where | Button | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Task** | speech bubble in the card header, next to the status | notes on the content, date, way of doing it |
| **Requirement** | speech bubble next to the requirement in “Show selected requirements in tasks” | e.g. “this requirement has no task yet” |
| **General notes** | section below the idea, above the tasks | notes on the whole advancement |

The number next to the bubble counts **open** notes (orange background). A grey number means all notes are resolved. On a computer a task's thread expands inside the card; on a phone it opens in a panel from the bottom of the screen.

![Comments on a phone](../img/komentarze-telefon.webp)

## For the mentor {#dla-opiekuna}

- Open the mentee's advancement and tap the bubble next to a task or requirement. Write the note and choose “Send” (on a computer also Ctrl+Enter, on a Mac ⌘+Enter).
- Above the tasks the **Open notes** panel lists every unresolved matter of this advancement. “Show” takes you to the right place and opens the thread.
- On the advancement list every advancement with open notes has an orange badge, e.g. “2 open notes”.

## For the mentee {#dla-podopiecznego}

- Reply in the same thread – threads are flat, entries go one after another.
- When you have fixed what the note was about, choose **Resolved ✓** next to it. Resolved notes fold under “Show resolved (n)”.
- “Reopen” brings a note back if the matter returns.

Anyone can mark a note as resolved – just as anyone with the room link can change the data. Agree with your mentor who does it.

## Writing as {#pisze-jako}

The app has no accounts, so before your first comment it asks: **“Who writes on this device?”**. The mentor and mentee of this advancement are at the top. Not on the list? Choose “Add person”.

The choice is remembered **on this device only**. Change it with “change” below the comment field or in [Settings](/pomoc/ustawienia#pisze-jako). The “Mentor” / “Mentee” label comes from the advancement's settings, so it updates when the advancement's mentor changes.

## Why can I only edit my own comments {#dlaczego-moge-edytowac-tylko-swoje-komentarze}

“Edit” and “Delete” are shown only on comments by the person chosen in “Writing as” on this device. This is **a guard against mistakes, not a security feature**: anyone with the room link can pick any person. An edited comment is marked “edited”.

## The “new” dot {#kropka-nowe}

A red dot on the bubble and a “new” tag on an entry mark comments by other people written since your last visit to this advancement. A visit lasts until you leave the advancement or close the app, so the dots do not disappear while you are still reading. The app sends no notifications – there is no server to send them.

## Link to a thread {#link-do-watku}

“Copy link” below a thread copies an address that opens the advancement right at this thread, e.g. `…/stopien/…#zadanie-…`. You can send it in a messenger. The link works on devices in the same [sync room](/pomoc/synchronizacja).

## Comments in the PDF and in the export {#komentarze-w-pdf-i-w-eksporcie}

Comments are **not included in the PDF** unless you tick “Include comments” in the preview – see [Preview and PDF](/pomoc/pdf#komentarze-w-pdf). The JSON export always contains them.

> [!NOTE]
> Changing the advancement's rank deletes the comments on the previous rank's requirements, and deleting a task deletes its comments. The confirmation tells you beforehand.
